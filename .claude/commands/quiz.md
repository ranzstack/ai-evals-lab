Quiz me on module **$ARGUMENTS** (if no number is given, use the most recent module whose lesson is done but whose quiz isn't).

- Read the module file. Write 5 questions that mix: 2 conceptual ("why…"), 2 applied (a short scenario, e.g. a results table or an eval design to critique), and 1 lead-engineer judgment call (a team or process situation with no single right answer).
- Ask **one at a time** and wait for my answer. Then give feedback: what was right, what was missing, and the key idea in one sentence.
- Score 0–2 per question (judgment questions are scored on reasoning quality, not on a "correct" answer).
- At the end, give my score out of 10, name the weakest concept, and suggest a fix (re-read a section, or a specific lab step). Record the score in `PROGRESS.md`.
